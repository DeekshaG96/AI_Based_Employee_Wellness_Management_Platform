Confidential Support Helplines

MoodMentor is a self-reflection tool, not a clinical emergency service. If you or someone you know is experiencing severe distress or crisis, compassionate professional support is available 24/7 free of charge:

United States & Canada: Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
United Kingdom: Call 111 (NHS Mental Health Services) or text SHOUT to 85258.
India: Call 14416 (Tele-MANAS National Helpline) or Vandrevala Foundation (9999 666 555).
Worldwide / International: Visit befrienders.org or iasp.info for local emergency support.
