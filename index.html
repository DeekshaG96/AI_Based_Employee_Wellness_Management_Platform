Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MoodMentor's architecture and privacy.

How does the 15-second daily check-in work? ▼
Employees simply pick their primary mood rating on a 5-point spectrum (Burned Out to Radiant Joy), select relevant feeling chips that dynamically adjust to their mood, tag key workplace contributors, and optionally type reflections for real-time AI emotion scoring.
How does MoodMentor protect employee privacy and data isolation? ▼
All passwords and sensitive tokens are encrypted using salted PBKDF2 with SHA-256 over 120,000 iterations. Individual reflection logs are strictly scoped by user ID and private session tokens, ensuring confidentiality while aggregating macro trends for leadership.
Why use both VADER and Hugging Face Transformer models together? ▼
VADER provides instantaneous, deterministic valence scoring tuned specifically for lexical capitalization, punctuation intensifiers, and emoticons. The DistilRoBERTa transformer provides fine-grained emotional depth across 7 distinct states. Together, they deliver rapid polarity assessment and deep psychological insight.
Is MoodMentor a medical or clinical diagnostic tool? ▼
No. MoodMentor is explicitly designed as a self-reflection, mindfulness, and proactive workplace well-being platform. It does not diagnose mental health conditions. If severe distress or self-harm keywords are detected, the system immediately surfaces regional crisis hotlines and accredited professional support resources.
