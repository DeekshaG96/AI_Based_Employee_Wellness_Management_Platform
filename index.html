Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MoodMentor's architecture and privacy.

How does MoodMentor protect employee privacy and data isolation? ▼
All passwords are encrypted using salted PBKDF2 with SHA-256 over 120,000 iterations. Relational records in SQLite are strictly scoped by user ID, meaning reflections and mood logs cannot be cross-referenced or accessed without explicit authorization.
Why use both VADER and Hugging Face Transformer models together? ▼
VADER provides instantaneous, deterministic valence scoring tuned specifically for lexical capitalization, punctuation intensifiers, and emoticons. The DistilRoBERTa transformer provides fine-grained emotional depth across 7 distinct states (Joy, Sadness, Fear, Anger, Surprise, Disgust, Neutral). Together, they deliver rapid polarity assessment and deep psychological insight without latency bottlenecks.
Is MoodMentor a medical or clinical diagnostic tool? ▼
No. MoodMentor is explicitly designed as a self-reflection, mindfulness, and proactive workplace well-being platform. It does not diagnose mental health conditions. If severe distress or self-harm keywords are detected, the system immediately surfaces regional crisis hotlines and accredited professional support resources.
Can teams process batch pulse surveys or CSV files? ▼
Yes. The platform provides automated CSV and text ingestion with feedback column auto-detection. It can evaluate hundreds of rows in batch, compute aggregated emotion distributions, and generate longitudinal trend charts.
